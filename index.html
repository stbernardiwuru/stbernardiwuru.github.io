ABOUT ST. BERNARD MONASTERY

St. Bernard Benedictine Monastery is a contemplative community of Roman Catholic monks located in Iwuru Central, Cross River State, Nigeria. Called by God to live the Gospel in the cloistered tradition of Saint Benedict of Nursia, the monastic brotherhood is consecrated to a life of perpetual praise, intercessory prayer, humble manual work, and peaceful fraternal fellowship.

Following the ancient motto "Ora et Labora" (Prayer and Work), our daily rhythm is sanctified by the Divine Office and Holy Mass, alternating with intellectual study and farm labor to sustain our life of quiet dedication.

THE CHRONICLE OF ST. BERNARD BENEDICTINE MONASTERY

The planting of a Benedictine monastery in Iwuru Central, Cross River State represents a historic commitment to contemplative witness in Nigeria. Every monastery begins through prayerful perseverance and the generous providence of Almighty God operating through church leaders and faithful benefactors.

Under the ecclesiastical jurisdiction of the Catholic Diocese of Ogoja Province and guided by the timeless statutes of the Order of Saint Benedict (O.S.B.), St. Bernard Monastery was founded to be an oasis of spiritual calm, offering unceasing prayer for the nation and cultivating a sanctuary where souls can draw closer to the divine presence.

From humble beginnings of land acquisition and infrastructural setup, the community continues to grow in monastic vocations, physical structure, and spiritual depth, serving as a beacon of faith in Cross River State.

The Journey of Benedictine Discernment

Discerning a monastic vocation is a peaceful, patient process of listening to God's gentle invitation. It is founded upon regular prayer, spiritual direction, and an open heart willing to leave worldly preoccupations behind for the surpassing treasure of Christ.

Catholic men who feel drawn to a life of prayer, silence, manual labor, and community living under the Rule of Saint Benedict are welcome to inquire about visiting our monastery for discernment retreats.

Basic Requirements for Inquiry:

  • Catholic gentleman in good standing with the Church.
  • Possession of good physical and psychological health.
  • Minimum educational qualification (Secondary School Certificate or equivalent).
  • Deep love for prayer, silence, and community life.
